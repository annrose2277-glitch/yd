Common questions

How do I register for an event?

Registration opens through the official Yagna Dhruva channels and event pages. If you can't find your event listed or the link isn't working, call the relevant coordinator above and they'll register you directly.

I haven't received my confirmation — who do I contact?

Reach out to the coordinator for your event category — Yagna coordinators for technical events, Dhruva coordinators for cultural events. Keep your name and event name ready when you call.

Where do I get sponsorship or corporate tie-up details?

All sponsorship packages, stall bookings and advertisement queries go through Corporate Relations. Call or message Adithya or Gauri above for the full sponsorship deck.

What time do events start each day?

Timings vary by event and day across Oct 8–10. Check the specific event listing first; if it's unclear, the coordinator for that category can confirm your slot.
